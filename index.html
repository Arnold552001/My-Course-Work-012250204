Why paying taxes matters

Taxes paid for roads, schools, health centres and water. When you earn a salary or run a business, paying your share keeps these services running. Being registered also helps you open a business account, apply for loans and keep clean records.

Steps to Register for a TIN

  1. Gather your National ID (NIN), phone number and email.
  2. Open the online TIN registration page on the official URA website.
  3. Choose individual or business and fill in the form.
  4. Check your details and submit.
  5. Save or print your TIN certificate.
